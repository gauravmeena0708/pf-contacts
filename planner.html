How gaps are calculated

Candidates come from Voronoi centres at least 50 km inside the mainland boundary. A candidate is a gap when its nearest EPFO office is farther away than the selected service radius; results within 50 km of a larger gap are consolidated.

Boundary: Natural Earth 5.1.1, India point of view, mainland polygon only. Dataset details.
